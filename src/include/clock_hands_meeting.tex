\section {Clock hands meeting (Algebra)}

How many times a day do the hour hand and the minute hand of a clock meet?

\subsection{Solution}

If you thought it was 24, you are mistaken: they meet 22 times.

The hands meet at 12:00, then a little after 1:05, then a little after 2:10 and so on.
But between 11:00 and 12:00 they do not meet, since the meeting that would be ``a little after 11'' is the meeting at 12:00.
So in 12 hours they meet 11 times (counting the 12:00 at the start but not the 12:00 at the end), and in a day they meet 22 times.

Another way to count: in a day the minute hand goes around the clock 24 times and the hour hand twice.
Every meeting is a time when the minute hand overtakes the hour hand by a full lap, and the minute hand gains $24-2=22$ laps in a day.
So they meet 22 times, every $\frac{24}{22} = 1\frac{1}{11}$ hours.
