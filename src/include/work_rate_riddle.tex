\section {Working together (Algebra)}

Alice and Bob together do a certain job in $2$ hours.
Alice and Charlie together do it in $3$ hours.
Bob and Charlie together do it in $4$ hours.
How long would it take Alice, Bob and Charlie to do the job together?

\subsection{Solution}

Let $A$, $B$ and $C$ be the parts of the job that Alice, Bob and Charlie do in an hour. Then
\begin{center}
$A+B = \frac{1}{2}$, \quad $A+C = \frac{1}{3}$, \quad $B+C = \frac{1}{4}$
\end{center}
There is no need to find $A$, $B$ and $C$ separately. Adding the three equations, every person appears twice:
\begin{center}
$2(A+B+C) = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{13}{12}$
\end{center}
so $A+B+C = \frac{13}{24}$. Working together they do $\frac{13}{24}$ of the job every hour, so the job takes them
$\frac{24}{13}$ hours, which is about $1$ hour and $51$ minutes.
